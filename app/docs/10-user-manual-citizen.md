# NavoPulse User Manual: Residents

NavoPulse is the City of Navotas app for emergencies, community reports, city advisories, and questions to NavoBot. It works in Filipino and English.

> **In a life-threatening emergency, call 911 right away.** NavoPulse helps the city find and respond to you, but it does not replace a phone call when every second counts.

> Note for this version: the citizen app is a web app (PWA) at `/app` that you can install on your phone. A separate Play Store / App Store app is planned for a later version. Screen labels below follow the app's text; small differences are possible while the app is being finished.

## 1. Getting started

### 1.1 Open and install
1. On your phone, open the NavoPulse link given by the City (for example `https://<city-domain>/app`) in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone).
2. Install it:
   - **Android (Chrome):** tap the menu ⋮ → **Add to Home screen** / **Install app**.
   - **iPhone (Safari):** tap Share → **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open NavoPulse from the new icon.

### 1.2 Choose your language
Tap **Language / Wika** and choose Filipino or English. Filipino is the default.

### 1.3 Sign in with your mobile number
1. Tap **Mag-sign in / Sign in**.
2. Enter your mobile number (e.g. `0917 123 4567`) and tap **Ipadala ang code / Send code**.
3. You get a 6-digit code by SMS. Enter it and tap **I-verify / Verify**. The code works for 5 minutes. If it expires, request a new one (up to 3 times every 15 minutes).
4. **First time only:** enter your name, choose your barangay, and read the **Privacy Notice**. Tick "Nabasa ko ang Privacy Notice at pumapayag ako…" to create your account. Your barangay decides which local advisories you receive.

Never share your code with anyone, including people who say they are from the City. NavoBot and City staff will never ask for it.

### 1.4 Guest mode
Tap **Magpatuloy bilang bisita / Continue as guest** to see advisories and city information without an account. You need to sign in to send reports.

## 2. Emergency SOS

1. On Home, tap the big red **SOS** button.
2. Choose what is happening: **Baha / Flood**, **Sunog / Fire**, **Medikal / Medical**, or **Iba pa / Other**.
3. Allow location when asked. The app shows "Kinukuha ang iyong lokasyon… / Getting your location…". If it cannot find you, move the pin on the map to where you are.
4. Optional: add a short description ("may matanda at bata, nasa second floor"), the number of people affected, and a photo.
5. Tap **Ipadala ang ulat / Send report**.
6. You see **Naipadala ang ulat / Report sent** and a **control number** like `NVP-EMR-20261019-000123`. Keep it.

The City Command Center sees your SOS on its map within seconds.

If the pin is outside Navotas, the app asks you to move it to where the incident is.

### 2.1 No internet? Use SMS
If you have no mobile data:
- The app says "Walang koneksyon. Naka-save ang ulat at kusang ipapadala." Your report is saved on the phone and sent automatically when data comes back.
- For faster help, tap **Ipadala sa SMS / Send via SMS instead**. The app opens your messages app with a ready-made text to the City's emergency SMS number. Just press **Send**. Normal SMS charges may apply.
- The text looks like `NVP1 SOS FLD 14.66712,120.94231 P3 R… N:Stranded sa bubong`. Do not change the numbers.
- You will get an SMS reply with your control number. If the same report later goes through the internet, it will not be counted twice.

If you cannot send an SMS either, call 911.

## 3. Report a concern (DisipliNavo Cam)

Use this for non-emergencies: illegal parking, vehicles blocking an emergency route, road obstructions, accidents, garbage dumping, clogged drainage, broken streetlights, damaged roads or bridges, and other concerns.

1. On Home, tap **Mag-ulat ng problema / Report a concern**.
2. Choose the category.
3. Tap **Magdagdag ng larawan / Add photo** to take or choose a photo (up to 5).
   - If the photo has location information, the app uses it and shows **Lokasyon mula sa larawan / Location taken from photo**. Otherwise it uses your phone's GPS. You can move the pin.
4. Add a short description and tap **Send report**.
5. You get a control number starting with `NVP-CIV-` (civic) or `NVP-TRF-` (traffic).

Tips: take the photo where the problem is; include a landmark; do not photograph faces or plate numbers more than needed.

## 4. Track your reports

1. Tap **Aking mga ulat / My reports**.
2. Each report shows its status:

| Status (FIL / EN) | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Naisumite / Submitted | Received by the City |
| Napatunayan / Verified | Checked by the office |
| Naitalaga / Assigned | A response team has been assigned |
| Inaaksyunan / In Progress | The team is working on it |
| Nalutas / Resolved | Done; you can see the resolution photo |
| Tinanggihan / Rejected | Not acted on (for example, a test or not within the City's scope) |
| Doble / Duplicate | Someone else already reported it; follow the other control number shown |

3. Tap a report to see its history and, when resolved, the **resolution photo**.

You also get a notification (and an SMS if notifications are off or you have no data) whenever the status changes. Allow notifications when the app asks.

## 5. Rate the service
When a report is **Resolved**, the app asks **Kumusta ang aming serbisyo? / How was our service?** Tap 1 to 5 stars, add an optional comment, and send. You can change your rating later. Ratings help the City improve.

## 6. Ask NavoBot
1. Tap **Tanungin si NavoBot / Ask NavoBot**.
2. Type your question in Filipino, English or Taglish, e.g.:
   - "Ano ang requirements sa business permit renewal?"
   - "Saan ang evacuation center sa Tanza 1?"
   - "Paano pumunta sa Divisoria?"
   - "Mag-report ng basura sa San Jose"
3. NavoBot answers from official City documents only. If it does not know, it will say so; contact the office it names.
4. NavoBot can file a report for you. It asks what happened, where (share location or say your barangay), and optionally your name and number. It gives you a control number.

NavoBot cannot send rescuers. In an emergency it will tell you to press SOS or call 911.

## 7. City information

| Section | What you find |
|---|---|
| **Mga Abiso / Advisories** | Flood, high-tide and weather advisories for the whole city and your barangay. Critical alerts may also come by SMS. |
| **Transport Hub** | Jeepney, bus and tricycle routes, terminals and minimum fares, with a map. Fares can change; check the posted fare matrix. |
| **Direktoryo ng Barangay / Barangay Directory** | The 18 barangays with hall address, hotline, officials and map location. |
| **Angat Navotas** | City announcements, upcoming events and local businesses. |

## 8. Privacy
- Your mobile number is stored encrypted. Only authorised City staff who need it can see it, and every view is recorded.
- Your reports are visible only to you and the City offices handling them.
- Read the full Privacy Notice in the app. To ask for a copy, correction or deletion of your data, contact the City's Data Protection Officer listed there.

## 9. Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| No code received | Wait 1 minute, check the number, request again (max 3 per 15 minutes). |
| "Incorrect code" | Re-type it; after 5 wrong tries, request a new code. |
| "Too many requests" | Wait the number of minutes shown. |
| Location not found | Turn on location (GPS) in phone settings, go near a window or outside, or move the pin by hand. |
| Report stuck "waiting to send" | It will send when you have data. For emergencies use SMS or call 911. |
| No notifications | Allow notifications for the app/browser; you will still get SMS for status changes. |
| Photo rejected | Use a JPG/PNG photo under 5 MB. |
